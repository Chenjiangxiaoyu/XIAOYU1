# Days 12–21 — Retargeting, Practice, and Local Learning Workflow

This document consolidates the implementation record for Days 12–21. Day 17 did not retain a separate daily document, so its completed functionality is included here.

## Day 12 — Pose-to-Avatar Retargeting

`PoseRetargeter` maps the processed reference `PoseSequence` to the simple Three.js teaching avatar. Shoulder-to-wrist and hip-to-ankle vectors determine limb directions, while the shoulder midpoint relative to the hip midpoint determines torso lean. This is a minimal limb-and-torso mapping, not production humanoid rigging, inverse kinematics, or foot-contact correction.

## Day 13 — Deterministic Real-Time Motion Scoring

`MotionScorer` compares the tracked camera pose with the active reference pose using body-centred, scale-normalized 3D landmark distance across shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles. The training page reports a 0–100 score, per-joint scores, and low-score feedback. It is local and deterministic: no LLM, camera recording, or biometric identification is used.

## Day 14 — Step Temporal Matching and Score Smoothing

`StepProgressMatcher` performs monotonic local search through the ordered reference frames for the active step. It matches live normalized pose against a short forward-only reference window, preventing common jitter from moving progress backward. The overall score uses an exponential smoothing blend of 70% previous score and 30% current score.

## Day 15 — Step Completion and Unlocking

A step passes only when matched progress is at least 90%, the smoothed score is at least 75/100, and both conditions persist for two seconds. Passing unlocks the next step and saves a browser-local training record containing score, progress, stable duration, and completion time. No camera video is stored.

## Day 16 — Local Training History and Report

The History page lists local passing records. The Report page summarizes unique passed steps, average passing score, total records, and the latest completion. Both pages read browser local storage only.

## Day 17 — Real-Time Corrective Coaching

`CorrectionCoach` converts the lowest deterministic joint score into a concise correction prompt. The same issue must persist for 1.2 seconds before a prompt is issued, and repeated speech is limited to once every five seconds. Browser-native speech is optional; no audio is recorded, transmitted, or generated by an LLM.

## Day 18 — Local Practice Recommendation

The report derives an explainable recommendation from local passing records. A lowest passing score below 82 recommends revisiting that step; otherwise it recommends continuing after the most recently completed step. This is rule-based, not an LLM judgement.

## Day 19 — Local Course Progress Persistence

Generated course metadata, unlocked-step count, and passed-step identifiers are stored in browser local storage and restored on the Course page. Source-video object URLs and processed reference PoseSequence data intentionally remain session-only; after a refresh, the reference video must be re-imported for playback and real-time scoring.

## Day 20 — Local Readiness Diagnostics

The Debug page provides a read-only checklist for WebGL, camera API availability, browser speech support, local storage, course availability, and temporary reference-video/Pose readiness. It never requests camera permission or changes data.

## Day 21 — End-to-End Integration and Acceptance

The Home page presents the integrated local workflow: MP4 analysis, course generation, reference or 3D demonstration, camera practice, deterministic scoring/coaching, unlocking, and reporting. The manual acceptance path is Debug → Analyze → Course → Training → History/Report. Production builds pass, while real-camera validation remains device-, permission-, lighting-, and source-video-dependent.

## Post-Day-21 Additions

Fast-motion analysis now uses a two-pass strategy: a 10 FPS coarse pass detects high normalized landmark displacement, and those intervals are resampled at 30 FPS before preprocessing, segmentation, and retargeting. Teaching playback presents a user-selectable slow-motion recommendation rather than forcing slow playback. The learner can also choose reference-video or 3D-avatar teaching mode, manually box-select the main subject before analysis, and use that selection to prioritize tracker initialization and establish the reference-video crop.
